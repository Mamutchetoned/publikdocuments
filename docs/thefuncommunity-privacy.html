Privacy Policy — TheFunCommunity

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Developer: Samuil Nedelchev  ·  Contact: snedelchev@gmail.com  ·  Last updated: 2026-06-28

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, date of birth, and profile photo when you register.
  • User-generated content: Forum posts, thread replies, event comments, place reviews, and direct messages you send within the app.
  • Usage data: App activity such as events you attend, places you visit, and leaderboard participation.
  • Device information: Basic device and OS information collected automatically by Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for performance monitoring.
  • Location: Approximate location when you search for nearby places (only when you grant permission).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide community features: forum, events, place reviews, leaderboard, and direct messaging.
  • To send push notifications about community activity (you can disable these in device settings).
  • To improve app stability and performance via crash reports.
  • To moderate content and enforce our community guidelines.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, storage, analytics, and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps: Used to display maps within the app. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Other users of the app can see your display name, profile photo, posts, reviews, and event attendance.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the app (Profile → Delete Account), which permanently removes your personal data from our systems within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

TheFunCommunity requires users to be at least 13 years old to register. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at snedelchev@gmail.com and we will delete the account promptly.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view your profile data within the app.
  • Correction: You can update your name and profile photo in the app.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account from Profile → Delete Account.
  • Data export: Contact us at snedelchev@gmail.com to request a copy of your data.

7. Security

We use Firebase's industry-standard security practices including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS) and Firebase Authentication for secure login. User passwords are never stored by us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
