Privacy Policy — DentalApp

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Developer: Samuil Nedelchev  ·  Contact: snedelchev@gmail.com  ·  Last updated: 2026-07-02

1. Information We Collect

  • Patient personal data: First name, last name, date of birth, phone number, email address, and home address, collected when a patient profile is created.
  • Health-related information: Dental treatment types, appointment history, clinical notes, and freehand drawing annotations attached to appointments or patient records.
  • Account credentials: Email and password used to authenticate with the app (stored and managed securely by Supabase Auth).
  • Appointment data: Dates, times, durations, treatment types, statuses, and any notes associated with scheduled appointments.
  • Calendar photos: Photos of paper appointment calendars that you choose to upload when using the "Import from photo" feature. These are stored privately and are only accessible to authorised dental staff.
  • Push notification tokens: A device token used to deliver appointment reminders and status updates to the app.
  • Device calendar data: Appointment events written to and read from your device's calendar when you grant calendar permission, to keep the app and device calendar in sync.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage patient profiles and dental appointment scheduling.
  • To send push notifications about appointment confirmations, reminders, and status changes.
  • To synchronise appointments with your device's calendar app.
  • To allow dental staff to add clinical notes and drawing annotations to patient records.
  • To process appointment requests submitted by patients through the patient portal.
  • To allow photos of paper calendars to be reviewed and converted into digital appointment drafts.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored in Supabase, a hosted cloud database with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS).
  • Access to patient records is protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies: patients can only view their own records; dental staff can manage all records within their practice.
  • Calendar scan photos are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket and are not publicly accessible.
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely on-device using Expo SecureStore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • The app does not log or transmit any data beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

4. Permissions Requested

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to photograph a paper appointment calendar for import. Not used for any other purpose.
  • Photo library: Used only when you choose to select a photo of your paper calendar from your library for import.
  • Calendar: Used to create and update appointment events in your device calendar so your schedule stays in sync. Requires your explicit approval.
  • Push notifications: Used to send appointment reminders and status updates. You can disable these at any time in your device settings.

5. Data Retention

  • Patient records and appointment history are retained for as long as the dental practice operates and requires them for clinical or legal purposes.
  • Calendar scan photos are retained until the import session is completed or deleted by dental staff.
  • If a patient requests anonymisation (available within the patient portal), their personal identifiers (name, phone, date of birth, email) are permanently removed from the system. Appointment history is retained in anonymised form.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: You may request a copy of the personal data held about you.
  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data. Patients can trigger anonymisation directly from the app's patient portal.
  • Right to restrict processing: You may request that we limit how your data is used.
  • Right to data portability: You may request your data in a portable format.
  • Right to object: You may object to certain types of processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at snedelchev@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

DentalApp is intended for use by dental practices and their adult patients. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. If a minor's data is entered as part of a patient record, it is handled with the same security measures as all other patient data, and parental or guardian consent is assumed to have been obtained by the dental practice.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
